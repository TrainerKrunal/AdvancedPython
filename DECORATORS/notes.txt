Decorators - Theory Notes
=========================

What are Decorators?
--------------------
Decorators are functions that modify the behavior of other functions or methods. They are often used for logging, access control, timing, and more.

Key Concepts:
- @decorator: Syntax to apply a decorator to a function.
- Wrapper Function: The function returned by a decorator that adds extra behavior.
- Chaining: Multiple decorators can be applied to a single function.

Use Case: Logging access to sensitive banking operations, checking user permissions, or timing function execution.
