Magic Methods - Theory Notes
============================

What are Magic Methods?
-----------------------
Magic methods (also called dunder methods) are special methods in Python with double underscores before and after their names (e.g., __init__, __str__).
 They enable custom behavior for built-in operations (like printing, addition, iteration).

Key Concepts:
- __init__: Object initializer (constructor).
- __str__, __repr__: String representations of objects.
- __add__, __sub__, etc.: Operator overloading.
- __len__, __getitem__, __setitem__: Make objects behave like collections.
- __iter__, __next__: Make objects iterable.

Use Case: Customizing how objects behave with Python's built-in functions and operators.
